# Code of Conduct

We want Media Renamer's community to be welcoming, constructive, and safe.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus discussion on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behaviour harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through [GitHub's security advisory form](https://github.com/manix84/media-renamer/security/advisories/new). Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
